If you plan to use the bikes often, a monthly membership costs twelve pounds and includes unlimited free rides under thirty minutes. All the bikes are checked and serviced regularly by our maintenance team, so you should always find them in good condition. If you do find a problem with a bike, please report it through the app, and our team will collect it within a few hours. Helmets are not provided with the bikes, so we recommend bringing your own if you plan to cycle regularly. The bikes can be returned to any station in the city, not just the one where you picked it up, which makes one-way trips very convenient. We also have several dedicated cycle lanes across the city centre, making cycling safer and more pleasant. During rush hour, some stations can become full, so the app shows you in real time which stations have free spaces to return your bike. If a station is full, you have an extra ten minutes free of charge to find another nearby station. The scheme has proven very popular since it launched two years ago, with over five thousand rides recorded every single week. We're always looking for feedback, so please use the app to let us know how we can improve the service. We are also planning to introduce electric bikes next year for longer journeys and hillier parts of the city, which should make the scheme even more popular. Students can apply for a discounted membership at half price, simply by showing a valid student card at any of our information points. We work closely with local schools to teach cycling safety to children during the summer holidays, which has proven very successful so far. Thank you for listening, and we hope you'll give the bike-share scheme a try soon.
