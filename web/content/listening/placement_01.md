NARRATOR: Section 1. You will hear a phone conversation between a customer and a staff member at a public library. First, you have some time to look at questions 1 to 10.
B: Good afternoon, Riverside Public Library, this is Priya speaking. How can I help you?

B: Of course. Could I take your full name first?
A: Yes, it's Daniel Holt. That's H, O, L, T.
B: Thanks, Daniel. And what's your home address?
A: I live at fourteen Maple Street, just near the train station.
B: Great, and could I get a contact email address for you?
A: Sure, it's daniel dot holt eight eight at gmail dot com.
B: Perfect. Now, we offer three types of membership: Standard, Student, and Senior. Which would you like?
A: I'm a full-time university student, so the Student membership, please.
B: Good choice — that one's cheaper. The annual membership fee is twelve pounds for students, instead of the usual twenty pounds for Standard members.
A: That sounds reasonable. What do I need to bring to collect my card?
B: You'll need to bring both photo ID and a proof of address, like a utility bill, when you come in.
A: No problem, I have both of those. How long will it take to get the card ready?
B: Your card will be ready for collection in five working days.
A: Great. And how many books can I borrow at once with a Student card?
B: Student members can borrow up to eight books at a time, for three weeks each.
A: That's plenty. One more thing — how will I know when the card is ready?
B: We'll send you an email once it's ready for collection.
A: Perfect. And what are your opening hours during the week?
B: We're open Monday to Friday, and we close at seven in the evening on weekdays.
A: That's really helpful, thank you.
B: You're welcome. Oh, one last thing — if a book is returned late, there's a charge of ten pence per day.
A: Got it, I'll be careful to return everything on time. Thanks so much, Priya.
B: You're welcome, Daniel. Enjoy the library!
