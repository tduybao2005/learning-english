F: Good afternoon, and thank you all for coming to hear about our Book Donation Drive. Let me explain exactly how it works, so you know what to do if you'd like to take part.
F: First of all, the donation drive runs every year during the month of September. You can bring your donated books to the library at any time during opening hours that month, and simply leave them in the blue donation box by the main entrance.
F: We accept most types of books — fiction, non-fiction, children's books, and cookbooks are all very welcome. However, we cannot accept textbooks that are more than ten years old, because the information inside is often out of date.
F: Once your books arrive, our team of volunteers checks each one carefully. Books in good condition are added to our shelves if we don't already have a copy, or they're sold at our small book sale to raise money for new titles.
F: The book sale itself takes place on the last Saturday of September, from nine in the morning until four in the afternoon, in the main hall on the ground floor. Prices are very low — most paperbacks cost just fifty pence, and hardback books cost one pound.
F: All the money raised goes directly towards buying brand new books for the children's section, which is always very popular with young readers.
F: If you would like to help as a volunteer during the donation drive, we're always looking for extra hands to sort books and set up the book sale. You can sign up for volunteering at the front desk, or by sending an email to volunteer at riverside library dot org.
F: Last year, the donation drive collected over two thousand books and raised more than six hundred pounds for the children's section, so we're hoping for an even bigger result this year.
F: We'll also be putting up posters around the local area a few weeks before September, so keep an eye out for those as a reminder of the exact dates.
F: If you have a large number of books to donate, more than one box, for example, please call the library in advance so we can arrange a convenient time for you to drop them off.
F: Thank you again for your interest, and we hope to see your donations, and perhaps some of you as volunteers too, this September.
