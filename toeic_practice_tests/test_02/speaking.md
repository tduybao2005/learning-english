# TOEIC Speaking — Test 02

**Time: ~20 minutes** — **Score: 0–200**

Bối cảnh: Harborview Grand Hotel.

---

## Questions 1–2: Read a Text Aloud

Đọc to đoạn văn bên dưới, thu âm trong 45 giây chuẩn bị + 45 giây đọc.

#### Q1

> Welcome to Harborview Grand Hotel's annual Staff Appreciation Dinner.
> Tonight, we celebrate the dedication and hard work of our entire team.
> Please enjoy the refreshments in the Grand Ballroom, and feel free to
> mingle with colleagues from every department. Dinner service will begin
> promptly at seven o'clock, so please take your seats a few minutes early.

> **Gợi ý phát âm:** nhấn trọng âm vào "Appreciation Dinner", "dedication",
> "refreshments"; ngắt hơi rõ sau "Grand Ballroom" và sau "every department";
> đọc "promptly at seven o'clock" với ngữ điệu xuống để nhấn thời gian cụ thể.

#### Q2

> Attention all guests: beginning next Monday, the rooftop pool will be
> open exclusively to hotel guests from six a.m. to ten p.m. Day passes for
> outside visitors should be arranged in advance with the concierge desk.
> Thank you for your cooperation as we work to maintain a relaxing
> environment for all of our guests.

> **Gợi ý phát âm:** nhấn "exclusively", "hotel guests"; ngắt hơi sau
> "Attention all guests:" và sau "concierge desk."; giữ ngữ điệu trang
> trọng, đều, tốc độ vừa phải như một thông báo nội bộ.

## Questions 3–4: Describe a Picture

Mô tả bức tranh trong 45 giây (30 giây chuẩn bị).

#### Q3

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Sảnh lễ tân (lobby) sang trọng của một khách sạn lớn: ở
> giữa là một quầy lễ tân bằng gỗ dài, hai nhân viên đang làm thủ tục
> check-in cho một cặp khách. Bên trái, gần cửa kính lớn, một người khuân
> vác hành lý (bellhop) đang đẩy xe chở vali. Bên phải là một khu vực ngồi
> chờ với ghế sofa êm ái và một chiếc bàn cà phê, nơi hai người khách khác
> đang trò chuyện, tay cầm ly nước chào mừng.

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

In this picture, I can see the elegant lobby of a large hotel. In the
center of the room, two staff members are standing behind a long wooden
front desk, checking in a couple who just arrived. On the left side, near a
large glass entrance, a bellhop appears to be pushing a luggage cart loaded
with suitcases. On the right side of the picture, there's a small waiting
area with comfortable sofas and a coffee table, where two other guests seem
to be chatting while holding welcome drinks. Overall, the lobby looks
bright, modern, and welcoming.

#### Q4

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Một hội chợ du lịch (travel expo) trong một trung tâm
> hội nghị lớn: nhiều gian hàng (booth) với biểu ngữ (banner) đầy màu sắc,
> đại diện các khách sạn đứng phát tài liệu quảng cáo cho khách tham quan.
> Ở giữa lối đi, một nhóm khách đang xếp hàng để nhận quà tặng (túi vải in
> logo) tại một gian hàng lớn có biển "Harborview Grand Hotel". Phía xa là
> một sân khấu nhỏ nơi một diễn giả đang thuyết trình.

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

This picture shows a travel expo being held inside a large convention
center. There are several colorful booths with banners, and hotel
representatives are handing out promotional materials to visitors walking
through the aisles. In the middle of the picture, a group of attendees
appears to be lining up to receive free gifts, possibly branded tote bags,
at a large booth with a sign that reads "Harborview Grand Hotel." In the
background, there seems to be a small stage where a speaker is giving a
presentation to a seated audience. The atmosphere looks busy and energetic,
which is typical of a large travel industry event like this.

## Questions 5–7: Respond to Questions

Chủ đề khảo sát: thói quen đi du lịch và đặt phòng khách sạn.

#### Q5

**Question:** How often do you stay at hotels when you travel?

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

I stay at hotels a few times a year, usually during vacations or business
trips.

#### Q6

**Question:** What matters most to you when choosing a hotel — location,
price, or amenities?

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

Location matters most to me. I prefer hotels close to attractions or public
transportation, even if they cost a little more.

#### Q7

**Question:** Describe a memorable hotel stay you have had and explain why
it stood out to you.

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

A memorable hotel stay I had was at a small beachfront resort. The staff
remembered my name after the first day and upgraded my room without being
asked, which made the stay feel very personal. The breakfast was also
excellent, with fresh local ingredients every morning. Because of that
experience, I would definitely recommend the hotel to friends and consider
staying there again.

## Questions 8–10: Respond Using Information Provided

Sử dụng lịch trình bên dưới để trả lời các câu hỏi. Bạn có 45 giây chuẩn bị
trước khi nghe ba câu hỏi.

**Harborview Grand Hotel — Group Check-In Schedule (October 12)**

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 9:00 a.m. | Registration and Welcome Drinks |
| 9:30 a.m. | Group Check-In — Rooms 300–320 |
| 10:00 a.m. | Group Check-In — Rooms 321–340 |
| 11:30 a.m. | Luggage Delivery to Rooms |
| 1:00 p.m. | Welcome Lunch in the Garden Terrace |
| 2:00 p.m. | Orientation Tour of Hotel Facilities |
| 3:00 p.m. | Free Time / Pool Access |

#### Q8

**Question:** What time does the check-in schedule begin, and what happens
first?

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

The schedule begins at 9:00 a.m. with registration and welcome drinks. That
is the first activity on the schedule.

#### Q9

**Question:** I'm staying in Room 335. What time should I check in, and
what happens right after that?

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

Since your room is between 321 and 340, you should check in at 10:00 a.m.
Right after that, at 11:30 a.m., your luggage will be delivered to your
room.

#### Q10

**Question:** Can you tell me about the schedule for the afternoon,
starting from lunch?

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

Sure. The welcome lunch is at 1:00 p.m. in the Garden Terrace. After that,
at 2:00 p.m., there's an orientation tour of the hotel facilities. Finally,
guests have free time and pool access starting at 3:00 p.m.

#### Q11 — Express an Opinion

**Question:** Some people believe that hotels should invest most of their
budget in modern technology, such as mobile check-in and smart rooms, while
others believe hotels should invest more in staff training to improve
personal service. Which do you think is better, and why?

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

In my opinion, hotels should invest more of their budget in staff training
rather than technology alone. First, personal service is often what guests
remember most about a stay; a warm greeting or a thoughtful gesture from a
staff member creates an emotional connection that technology cannot easily
replicate. Second, well-trained staff can handle unexpected problems, such
as complaints or special requests, far more effectively than an automated
system. Finally, while modern technology like mobile check-in can improve
convenience, it should support staff rather than replace them, since guests
still value human interaction, especially during important occasions like
weddings or anniversaries. For these reasons, I believe staff training
should be the priority, although some investment in technology is still
useful for convenience.
