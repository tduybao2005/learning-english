# TOEIC Writing — Test 02

**Time: ~60 minutes** — **Score: 0–200**

Bối cảnh: Harborview Grand Hotel.

---

## Questions 1–5: Write a Sentence Based on a Picture

Với mỗi tranh, viết MỘT câu hoàn chỉnh sử dụng CẢ HAI từ khóa cho sẵn. Bạn có
thể thay đổi hình thái từ (chia động từ, số nhiều...) và trật tự từ, nhưng
không được bỏ từ khóa.

#### Q1

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Một nhân viên lễ tân đang đứng sau quầy, tay đưa chìa
> khóa phòng cho một vị khách đang mỉm cười.

**Keywords: reception / while**

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

The receptionist handed over the room key while the guest smiled at the
front desk.

#### Q2

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Hai nhân viên bếp đang đứng cạnh nhau nhìn vào một đĩa
> món ăn, một người chỉ tay vào cách trang trí món ăn.

**Keywords: because / dish**

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

The chef pointed at the dish because she wanted to adjust the garnish.

#### Q3

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Một người khuân vác hành lý đang đẩy một xe chở vali
> lớn vào sảnh khách sạn.

**Keywords: deliver / luggage**

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

The bellhop delivered the guests' luggage to the front desk.

#### Q4

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Một nhóm khách du lịch đang đứng trước quầy lễ tân
> trong giờ cao điểm, tay cầm hộ chiếu và vali.

**Keywords: although / crowded**

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

Although the lobby was crowded, the tourists still waited patiently to
check in.

#### Q5

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Một nhân viên trực đêm đang ngồi làm việc muộn ở quầy
> lễ tân, ánh đèn bàn sáng, đồng hồ trên tường chỉ 11 giờ đêm.

**Keywords: because / late**

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

He was still working because a large group was checking in late, so he
stayed at the desk.

## Questions 6–7: Respond to a Written Request

Viết email trả lời trong khoảng 80 từ, đáp ứng đầy đủ yêu cầu trong đề bài.

#### Q6

**Đề bài:** Bạn nhận được email sau từ một khách hàng.

> Subject: Question about Upcoming Stay
>
> Hello,
>
> I have two quick questions about my upcoming stay. First, is early
> check-in possible if I arrive at noon instead of 3 p.m.? Second, is it
> possible to add a crib to the room for my infant?
>
> Also, could you send me the contact information for the concierge, in
> case I have quick questions during my stay?
>
> Thanks,
> Karen Whitfield

**Yêu cầu:** Trả lời với 2 câu hỏi ĐÃ được giải đáp và 1 đề nghị (offer/suggestion).

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

Dear Ms. Whitfield,

Thank you for reaching out. Early check-in at noon is possible, subject to
room availability on the day of arrival. Yes, we can add a crib to your
room at no extra charge if you let us know in advance.

I'd also suggest calling the concierge desk directly upon arrival so they
can personally confirm the crib setup — I can send you their contact
information right away if that works for you.

Best regards,
Nathan Cole

#### Q7

**Đề bài:** Bạn nhận được email sau từ một đồng nghiệp.

> Subject: Help with VIP Guest Arrival
>
> Hi,
>
> I'm preparing the welcome package for tomorrow's VIP guest arrival, but
> I'm not sure which amenities to include. Could you tell me which
> amenities impressed our last VIP guest the most? Also, do you know if
> this guest prefers a formal or casual welcome greeting?
>
> Could you also take a quick look at my package list before 5 p.m. today?
>
> Thanks,
> Jason

**Yêu cầu:** Trả lời với 2 câu hỏi ĐÃ được giải đáp và 1 đề nghị (offer/suggestion).

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

Hi Jason,

Our last VIP guest was most impressed by the fresh fruit basket and the
handwritten welcome note, so I'd include those again. As for the greeting
style, this guest generally prefers a formal welcome with the general
manager present rather than a casual approach.

I can review your package list around 4 p.m. if that works — just send it
over whenever it's ready, and I'll suggest any changes before your
deadline.

Best,
Priya

#### Q8 — Opinion Essay

**Đề bài:** Some people believe that excellent customer service is the most
important factor in a business's success, while others believe that price
or product quality matters more. Which viewpoint do you agree with? Use
specific reasons and examples to support your opinion. Write at least 300
words.

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

In today's competitive marketplace, businesses often debate what truly
drives their success: outstanding customer service, competitive pricing, or
superior product quality. While all three factors matter, I believe that
excellent customer service is the most important factor, mainly because it
builds lasting loyalty, differentiates a business from competitors, and
directly shapes how customers remember their experience.

First, customer service builds lasting loyalty in a way that price alone
cannot. Customers who feel genuinely cared for are far more likely to
return, even if a competitor offers a slightly lower price. For example, at
Harborview Grand Hotel, guests who receive personalized attention from
front desk staff, such as being remembered by name or having special
requests handled smoothly, often become repeat visitors and recommend the
hotel to friends and family. This kind of loyalty is difficult to achieve
through pricing strategies alone.

Second, in industries where products or services are similar, customer
service often becomes the main way a business stands out. Many hotels offer
comparable rooms, amenities, and rates, so the quality of interaction with
staff becomes the deciding factor for many travelers. A guest who
experiences a warm, attentive welcome is more likely to choose that hotel
again over a competitor with a nearly identical room but indifferent staff.

Third, customer service shapes how people remember their overall
experience, sometimes more than the product itself. A guest may forget the
exact thread count of the bed sheets, but they will remember whether staff
resolved a problem quickly and courteously. Poor service can ruin an
otherwise excellent product, while excellent service can even make up for
minor shortcomings elsewhere.

However, it is worth acknowledging that price and quality still matter
significantly. A business cannot survive on friendly service alone if its
prices are unreasonable or its products are unreliable. These factors work
together with service to create overall customer satisfaction.

In conclusion, while price and product quality remain important,
outstanding customer service has the greatest overall impact on a
business's success, because it builds loyalty, creates differentiation, and
defines how customers ultimately remember their experience. Businesses
should therefore prioritize training and empowering their staff to deliver
excellent service.
