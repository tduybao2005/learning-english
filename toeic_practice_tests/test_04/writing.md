# TOEIC Writing — Test 04

**Time: ~60 minutes** — **Score: 0–200**

Bối cảnh: Nimbus Cloud Systems, một công ty phần mềm và dịch vụ đám mây.

## Questions 1–5: Write a Sentence Based on a Picture

Với mỗi tranh, viết MỘT câu hoàn chỉnh sử dụng CẢ HAI từ khóa cho sẵn. Bạn có
thể thay đổi hình thái từ (chia động từ, số nhiều...) và trật tự từ, nhưng
không được bỏ từ khóa.

#### Q1

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Một lập trình viên đang ngồi trước hai màn hình máy
> tính, tay gõ bàn phím nhanh, trên màn hình hiển thị các dòng code.

**Keywords: while / code**

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

The developer reviewed the error log while writing new code.

#### Q2

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Hai kỹ sư đang đứng trước một bảng trắng, một người vẽ
> sơ đồ hệ thống, người còn lại chỉ tay vào một ô hình chữ nhật trên sơ đồ.

**Keywords: because / diagram**

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

The engineer pointed at the box because she wanted to revise the diagram.

#### Q3

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Một nhân viên giao hàng đang chuyển một thùng thiết bị
> máy chủ lớn cho nhân viên kỹ thuật trước cửa trung tâm dữ liệu.

**Keywords: deliver / server**

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

The courier delivered a new server to the data center technician.

#### Q4

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Một nhóm lập trình viên đang đứng quanh một chiếc bàn
> tại sự kiện hackathon, cầm cốc cà phê và nhìn vào máy tính bảng hiển thị
> điểm số.

**Keywords: although / crowded**

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

Although the hackathon venue was crowded, the team still focused on their
final demo.

#### Q5

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Một người đàn ông đang ngồi làm việc muộn trong văn
> phòng, ánh đèn màn hình sáng, đồng hồ trên tường chỉ 10 giờ tối.

**Keywords: because / deploy**

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

He was still working because the team needed to deploy the update before
midnight.

## Questions 6–7: Respond to a Written Request

Viết email trả lời trong khoảng 80 từ, đáp ứng đầy đủ yêu cầu trong đề bài.

#### Q6

**Đề bài:** Bạn nhận được email sau từ một khách hàng.

> Subject: Question about Migration Timeline
>
> Hello,
>
> I have two quick questions about our upcoming cloud migration. First,
> when will the staging environment be ready for us to test? Second, is it
> possible to schedule the migration for a weekend instead of a weekday?
>
> Also, could you send me the contact information for the engineer working
> on our project, in case I have quick questions during the week?
>
> Thanks,
> Grace Whitman

**Yêu cầu:** Trả lời với 2 câu hỏi ĐÃ được giải đáp và 1 đề nghị (offer/suggestion).

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

Dear Ms. Whitman,

Thank you for reaching out. The staging environment will be ready for
testing by next Wednesday. Yes, we can schedule the migration for a
weekend — our team already recommends Saturday night to minimize any
impact on your customers.

I'd also suggest a short call this week so our engineer, Sofia Wu, can
walk you through the migration plan directly — I can send you her contact
information right away if that works for you.

Best regards,
Priya Nair

#### Q7

**Đề bài:** Bạn nhận được email sau từ một đồng nghiệp.

> Subject: Help with Client Demo
>
> Hi,
>
> I'm preparing the slides for tomorrow's client demo, but I'm not sure
> which features to highlight. Could you tell me which features impressed
> the client most last time? Also, do you know if the client prefers a
> live demo or a recorded walkthrough?
>
> Could you also take a quick look at my slides before 5 p.m. today?
>
> Thanks,
> Ben

**Yêu cầu:** Trả lời với 2 câu hỏi ĐÃ được giải đáp và 1 đề nghị (offer/suggestion).

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

Hi Ben,

The client was most impressed by the new analytics panel and the faster
export speed from the last release, so I'd highlight those features. As
for format, they generally prefer a live demo so they can ask questions in
real time rather than a recorded walkthrough.

I can review your slides around 4 p.m. if that works — just send them over
whenever they're ready, and I'll suggest any changes before your deadline.

Best,
Lena

#### Q8 — Opinion Essay

**Đề bài:** Some people believe that technology, such as tablets and
online learning platforms, should play a central role in classroom
education, while others believe traditional teaching methods, such as
printed textbooks and face-to-face instruction, are more effective. Which
viewpoint do you agree with? Use specific reasons and examples to support
your opinion. Write at least 300 words.

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

Technology has become deeply embedded in nearly every part of modern life,
and education is no exception. While traditional teaching methods still
hold clear value, I believe that, for most students, giving technology a
central role in the classroom leads to better learning outcomes, mainly
because of personalized learning, greater access to resources, and
improved engagement.

First, technology allows instruction to be personalized in ways that
traditional classrooms often cannot achieve. Online learning platforms can
adjust the difficulty of exercises based on a student's performance,
letting fast learners move ahead while giving struggling students extra
practice on specific topics. For example, at Nimbus Cloud Systems, the
engineering team has built adaptive quiz tools for a partner school
district, and teachers there reported that students mastered difficult
math concepts more quickly once the software began targeting their
individual weak points.

Second, technology gives students access to a far wider range of
resources than a single textbook ever could. A tablet connected to the
internet can provide videos, interactive simulations, and up-to-date
information, all of which help explain complex topics from multiple
angles. This is especially valuable in subjects like science, where
students can watch simulations of experiments that would be too costly or
dangerous to perform in a physical classroom.

Third, many students find technology-based lessons more engaging than
traditional lectures. Interactive apps, gamified quizzes, and multimedia
content can hold students' attention longer than a printed page, which
often translates into better retention of the material and more active
participation in class discussions.

However, it is worth acknowledging that traditional methods still offer
benefits technology cannot fully replace, such as face-to-face guidance
from a teacher and the discipline of reading a full text without
distraction. Some skills, particularly deep, sustained reading, may
genuinely benefit from printed textbooks.

In conclusion, while traditional teaching methods support focus and
direct guidance, I believe technology offers greater overall benefits for
most students, thanks to personalization, broader access to resources, and
stronger engagement. Schools should therefore continue integrating
technology thoughtfully alongside proven traditional methods.
