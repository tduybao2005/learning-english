# TOEIC Writing — Test 03

**Time: ~60 minutes** — **Score: 0–200**

Bối cảnh: Meridian Freight & Logistics.

---

## Questions 1–5: Write a Sentence Based on a Picture

Với mỗi tranh, viết MỘT câu hoàn chỉnh sử dụng CẢ HAI từ khóa cho sẵn. Bạn có
thể thay đổi hình thái từ (chia động từ, số nhiều...) và trật tự từ, nhưng
không được bỏ từ khóa.

#### Q1

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Một nhân viên kho đang dùng máy quét mã vạch để kiểm
> tra một thùng hàng trên băng chuyền, mắt nhìn chăm chú vào màn hình máy
> quét.

**Keywords: scan / while**

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

The warehouse worker scanned the barcode while checking the box on the
conveyor belt.

#### Q2

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Hai tài xế đang đứng cạnh xe tải, một người chỉ tay
> vào bản đồ tuyến đường trên điện thoại, người kia gật đầu đồng ý.

**Keywords: because / route**

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

The driver pointed at the phone because he wanted to confirm the new
delivery route.

#### Q3

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Một xe nâng đang nâng một pallet chất đầy thùng
> carton lên kệ cao trong nhà kho, người lái xe nâng đội mũ bảo hộ.

**Keywords: lift / careful**

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

The forklift operator lifted the pallet carefully onto the top shelf.

#### Q4

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Một nhóm nhân viên đang đứng quanh một chiếc bàn, cùng
> nhau sắp xếp lại lịch giao hàng trên bảng trắng lớn.

**Keywords: although / crowded**

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

Although the office was crowded, the team still managed to reorganize the
delivery schedule together.

#### Q5

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Một nhân viên điều phối đang ngồi làm việc muộn trong
> văn phòng, nhiều màn hình theo dõi vị trí xe tải, đồng hồ trên tường
> chỉ 10 giờ tối.

**Keywords: because / late**

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

She was still tracking the trucks because a shipment had been delayed, so
she stayed late.

## Questions 6–7: Respond to a Written Request

Viết email trả lời trong khoảng 80 từ, đáp ứng đầy đủ yêu cầu trong đề bài.

#### Q6

**Đề bài:** Bạn nhận được email sau từ một khách hàng.

> Subject: Question about Shipment Status
>
> Hello,
>
> I have two quick questions about my shipment (order #4471). First, has
> it left the warehouse yet? Second, is it possible to change the
> delivery address to my office instead of my home?
>
> Also, could you send me the direct phone number for the dispatch team,
> in case I need to reach someone quickly this week?
>
> Thanks,
> Marisol Vega

**Yêu cầu:** Trả lời với 2 câu hỏi ĐÃ được giải đáp và 1 đề nghị (offer/suggestion).

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

Dear Ms. Vega,

Thank you for reaching out. Order #4471 left our warehouse this morning
and is currently in transit. Yes, we can update the delivery address to
your office as long as the change is confirmed before tomorrow's cutoff
time.

I'd also suggest saving our dispatch team's direct line — I can send it to
you right away, along with a tracking link so you can follow the
shipment's progress yourself.

Best regards,
Carlos Mendes

#### Q7

**Đề bài:** Bạn nhận được email sau từ một đồng nghiệp.

> Subject: Help with Route Planning
>
> Hi,
>
> I'm putting together tomorrow's delivery routes, but I'm not sure which
> stops to prioritize. Could you tell me which customers had complaints
> about late deliveries last week? Also, do you know if the new driver
> has finished the route-safety training yet?
>
> Could you also take a quick look at my draft schedule before 4 p.m.
> today?
>
> Thanks,
> Oscar Diaz

**Yêu cầu:** Trả lời với 2 câu hỏi ĐÃ được giải đáp và 1 đề nghị (offer/suggestion).

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

Hi Oscar,

Two customers on Elm Street and one on Harbor Road complained about late
deliveries last week, so I'd prioritize those stops early in the route. As
for the new driver, he completed the route-safety training on Monday, so
he's cleared to take on a full route.

I can review your draft schedule around 3 p.m. if that works — just send
it over whenever it's ready, and I'll flag anything that needs adjusting.

Best,
Renee

#### Q8 — Opinion Essay

**Đề bài:** Some people believe that employees accomplish more when they
work as part of a team, while others believe employees accomplish more
when they work individually. Which viewpoint do you agree with? Use
specific reasons and examples to support your opinion. Write at least 300
words.

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

Whether employees perform better working in teams or working alone is a
question that comes up in almost every industry, from creative fields to
logistics operations. While both approaches have clear benefits, I
believe that, for most complex tasks, employees accomplish more when they
work as part of a team, mainly because of shared expertise, faster
problem-solving, and mutual accountability.

First, teamwork allows employees to combine different areas of expertise
that no single person could provide alone. In a logistics company, for
example, planning an efficient delivery route often requires input from a
dispatcher who understands traffic patterns, a warehouse lead who knows
loading schedules, and a driver who is familiar with road conditions. When
these people work together, the resulting plan is usually far more
practical than one created by a single employee working in isolation.

Second, teams tend to solve unexpected problems more quickly than
individuals. When a shipment is delayed because of a mechanical issue or a
customs hold, for instance, a team can divide the work: one person
contacts the customer, another arranges a replacement truck, and a third
updates the tracking system, all at the same time. An individual handling
the same situation alone would need to complete each step in sequence,
which takes considerably longer.

Third, working in a team creates a sense of mutual accountability that
often improves the quality of work. Knowing that colleagues are relying on
them motivates employees to meet deadlines and double-check their work
more carefully than they might if no one else were involved in the
outcome.

However, it is worth acknowledging that individual work still has real
advantages, particularly for tasks that require deep, uninterrupted
concentration, such as reviewing detailed customs paperwork or analyzing
shipping data. Some employees may also produce more focused, higher
quality results when working without the distraction of constant group
discussion.

In conclusion, while individual work remains valuable for certain focused
tasks, I believe teamwork generally leads to greater overall
accomplishment, thanks to combined expertise, faster problem-solving, and
shared accountability. Companies should therefore continue to build
processes that encourage genuine collaboration rather than relying only on
individual effort.
