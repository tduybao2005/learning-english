## Questions 1–2: Read a Text Aloud

Đọc to đoạn văn bên dưới, thu âm trong 45 giây chuẩn bị + 45 giây đọc.

#### Q1

> Attention warehouse staff: starting this Monday, all outbound pallets
> must be scanned at the loading dock before they leave the building, no
> exceptions. This new step takes only a few seconds but will help us
> catch labeling errors before a shipment ever reaches the customer.
> Supervisors will hold a short refresher session on the scanners
> tomorrow morning for anyone who has questions.

> **Gợi ý phát âm:** nhấn trọng âm vào "Attention warehouse staff", "no
> exceptions", "labeling errors"; ngắt hơi rõ sau "loading dock" và sau
> "reaches the customer."; đọc câu cuối với ngữ điệu thân thiện, tốc độ vừa
> phải như một thông báo nội bộ.

#### Q2

> Thank you for choosing Meridian Freight & Logistics. Your shipment is
> now in transit and is expected to arrive within three to five business
> days. You can track its progress at any time using the tracking number
> provided in your confirmation email. If you have any questions about
> your delivery, our customer support team is available Monday through
> Saturday.

> **Gợi ý phát âm:** nhấn "in transit", "tracking number", "customer
> support"; ngắt hơi sau "Meridian Freight & Logistics." và sau "confirmation
> email."; giữ ngữ điệu ấm áp, rõ ràng như một tin nhắn xác nhận tự động.

## Questions 3–4: Describe a Picture

Mô tả bức tranh trong 45 giây (30 giây chuẩn bị).

#### Q3

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Một bến bốc xếp hàng hóa (loading dock) với ba xe tải
> đang đậu sát các cửa dock, cửa sau mở, công nhân dùng xe nâng (forklift)
> di chuyển các pallet hàng lên xe. Ở góc trái, một người mặc áo phản
> quang đang cầm bảng kẹp giấy (clipboard) kiểm tra danh sách hàng hóa.
> Phía sau, nhiều kệ hàng cao chứa các thùng carton xếp ngay ngắn.

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

In this picture, I can see a busy loading dock at a distribution center.
Three trucks are backed up against the dock doors, with their rear doors
open, while a worker operates a forklift to move pallets of goods onto one
of the trucks. On the left side of the picture, a person wearing a
reflective safety vest is holding a clipboard, apparently checking items
against a shipping list. In the background, there are tall rows of
shelving stacked with neatly organized cardboard boxes. Overall, the scene
looks organized and efficient, which is typical of a well-run logistics
operation.

#### Q4

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Bên trong một nhà kho lớn (warehouse) với trần cao và
> hệ thống kệ thép nhiều tầng. Một xe nâng đang nâng một pallet lên tầng
> kệ cao, người lái xe nâng đội mũ bảo hộ. Gần đó, hai nhân viên đang quét
> mã vạch (barcode scanner) trên các thùng hàng nhỏ hơn xếp trên xe đẩy.
> Ở phía xa, một nhân viên văn phòng đứng trước màn hình máy tính lớn
> theo dõi tình trạng hàng tồn kho.

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

This picture shows the inside of a large warehouse with tall ceilings and
multiple levels of steel shelving. A forklift operator wearing a hard hat
is lifting a pallet up to one of the higher shelves. Nearby, two employees
appear to be scanning barcodes on smaller boxes stacked on a cart. In the
background, there seems to be an office worker standing in front of a
large computer screen, likely monitoring inventory levels in real time.
The overall atmosphere looks busy but well organized, which suggests this
warehouse handles a large volume of goods every day.

## Questions 5–7: Respond to Questions

Chủ đề khảo sát: thói quen mua sắm và nhận hàng.

#### Q5

**Question:** How often do you order packages to be delivered to your
home?

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

I order packages to be delivered almost every week. Most of them are
online purchases like clothes or household items.

#### Q6

**Question:** Do you prefer picking up a package yourself or having it
delivered to your door?

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

I prefer having packages delivered to my door. It saves time and I don't
have to travel anywhere to collect them.

#### Q7

**Question:** Describe a time when a package you ordered was delayed and
explain how it affected you.

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

Once, I ordered a birthday gift online that was supposed to arrive in
three days, but it was delayed by almost a week due to a shipping issue.
I was quite frustrated because the birthday had already passed by the
time it arrived. I contacted customer support, and they offered a partial
refund for the inconvenience. Since then, I try to order gifts well in
advance to avoid similar problems.

#### Q11 — Express an Opinion

**Question:** Some people believe fast delivery speed is the most
important factor for customers when choosing a shipping company, while
others believe low shipping cost matters more. Which do you think matters
more, and why?

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

In my opinion, low shipping cost matters more to most customers than fast
delivery speed. First, many customers are not ordering urgent items, so
they are usually willing to wait a few extra days if it means saving
money on shipping fees. Second, for businesses that order in bulk, even a
small difference in shipping cost per unit can add up to a significant
amount over time, making cost a bigger factor in their decision than
speed. Finally, most online retailers already offer a range of delivery
options, so customers who truly need speed can simply choose and pay for
an express option when necessary, rather than expecting every shipment to
be fast by default. For these reasons, I believe cost is generally the
more important factor, although speed becomes critical for time-sensitive
deliveries such as perishable goods or urgent business documents.
