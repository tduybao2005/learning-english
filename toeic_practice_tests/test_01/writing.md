# TOEIC Writing — Test 01

**Time: ~60 minutes** — **Score: 0–200**

Bối cảnh: BrightWave Marketing & Advertising.

---

## Questions 1–5: Write a Sentence Based on a Picture

Với mỗi tranh, viết MỘT câu hoàn chỉnh sử dụng CẢ HAI từ khóa cho sẵn. Bạn có
thể thay đổi hình thái từ (chia động từ, số nhiều...) và trật tự từ, nhưng
không được bỏ từ khóa.

#### Q1

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Một nhân viên đang đứng thuyết trình trước một nhóm
> đồng nghiệp trong phòng họp, tay chỉ vào biểu đồ doanh số trên màn hình.

**Keywords: presentation / while**

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

The employee gave a presentation while her colleagues took notes.

#### Q2

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Hai nhân viên thiết kế đang ngồi cạnh nhau nhìn vào màn
> hình máy tính, một người chỉ tay vào một mẫu logo trên màn hình.

**Keywords: because / logo**

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

The designer pointed at the screen because she wanted to revise the logo.

#### Q3

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Một người đưa thư đang giao một thùng hàng lớn cho nhân
> viên lễ tân trước cửa văn phòng công ty.

**Keywords: deliver / package**

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

The courier delivered a large package to the receptionist.

#### Q4

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Một nhóm khách tham quan đang đứng trước một gian hàng
> tại hội chợ quảng cáo, cầm những tờ rơi màu sắc.

**Keywords: although / crowded**

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

Although the trade show was crowded, the visitors still lined up for the
flyers.

#### Q5

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Một người phụ nữ đang ngồi làm việc muộn trong văn
> phòng, ánh đèn bàn sáng, đồng hồ trên tường chỉ 9 giờ tối.

**Keywords: because / late**

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

She was still working because the campaign deadline was approaching, so she
stayed late.

## Questions 6–7: Respond to a Written Request

Viết email trả lời trong khoảng 80 từ, đáp ứng đầy đủ yêu cầu trong đề bài.

#### Q6

**Đề bài:** Bạn nhận được email sau từ một khách hàng.

> Subject: Question about Campaign Timeline
>
> Hello,
>
> I have two quick questions about our upcoming campaign. First, when will
> the first draft of the social media ads be ready? Second, is it possible
> to add a short video ad to the package we discussed?
>
> Also, could you send me the contact information for the designer working
> on our project, in case I have quick questions during the week?
>
> Thanks,
> Karen Whitfield

**Yêu cầu:** Trả lời với 2 câu hỏi ĐÃ được giải đáp và 1 đề nghị (offer/suggestion).

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

Dear Ms. Whitfield,

Thank you for reaching out. The first draft of the social media ads will be
ready by next Wednesday. Yes, we can add a short video ad to your package
for an additional fee, which I can outline in a follow-up email.

I'd also suggest scheduling a quick call this week so our designer, Mia
Tran, can walk you through the drafts directly — I can send you her contact
information right away if that works for you.

Best regards,
Daniel Reyes

#### Q7

**Đề bài:** Bạn nhận được email sau từ một đồng nghiệp.

> Subject: Help with Client Presentation
>
> Hi,
>
> I'm preparing the slides for tomorrow's client presentation, but I'm not
> sure which campaign results to highlight. Could you tell me which metrics
> impressed the client most last time? Also, do you know if the client
> prefers a formal or casual presentation style?
>
> Could you also take a quick look at my slides before 5 p.m. today?
>
> Thanks,
> Jason

**Yêu cầu:** Trả lời với 2 câu hỏi ĐÃ được giải đáp và 1 đề nghị (offer/suggestion).

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

Hi Jason,

The client was most impressed by the engagement rate and the increase in
website traffic from the last campaign, so I'd highlight those numbers. As
for style, they generally prefer a formal presentation with clear data
visuals rather than a casual tone.

I can review your slides around 4 p.m. if that works — just send them over
whenever they're ready, and I'll suggest any changes before your deadline.

Best,
Priya

#### Q8 — Opinion Essay

**Đề bài:** Some people believe that employees are more productive when they
work remotely, while others believe employees are more productive when they
work in a traditional office. Which viewpoint do you agree with? Use
specific reasons and examples to support your opinion. Write at least 300
words.

#### Gợi ý (Model answer)

In recent years, remote work has become increasingly common, and opinions
remain divided over whether it truly increases productivity compared to
traditional office work. While both approaches have advantages, I believe
that, for most employees, working remotely tends to result in higher
productivity, mainly because of reduced distractions, better time
management, and improved work-life balance.

First, working from home often eliminates many of the distractions common
in a traditional office environment. In an open-plan office, employees are
frequently interrupted by coworkers' conversations, unplanned meetings, or
general noise. At home, employees can create a quiet, personalized
workspace that allows them to focus more deeply on demanding tasks. For
example, at BrightWave Marketing, several copywriters have reported that
they can draft campaign scripts much faster when working from home, simply
because there are fewer interruptions throughout the day.

Second, remote work generally allows employees to manage their time more
effectively. Without a daily commute, workers save one or two hours each
day that can be used for either additional work or personal rest, both of
which contribute to better overall performance. Flexible schedules also
allow employees to work during the hours when they feel most productive,
rather than being restricted to a fixed nine-to-five schedule.

Third, remote work tends to improve employees' overall well-being, which in
turn affects their productivity. Employees who can balance their
professional and personal responsibilities more easily tend to experience
less stress and burnout. A less stressed employee is generally more
focused, more creative, and more willing to put in extra effort when
needed.

However, it is worth acknowledging that traditional office work still
offers benefits that remote work cannot fully replicate, such as
spontaneous collaboration and stronger team relationships. Some tasks,
particularly creative brainstorming sessions common in advertising agencies
like BrightWave Marketing, may genuinely benefit from face-to-face
interaction.

In conclusion, while traditional offices support collaboration, I believe
remote work offers greater overall productivity benefits for most
employees, thanks to fewer distractions, better time management, and
improved well-being. Companies should therefore consider offering flexible
remote options whenever possible.
