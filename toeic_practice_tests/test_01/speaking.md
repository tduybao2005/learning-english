# TOEIC Speaking — Test 01

**Time: ~20 minutes** — **Score: 0–200**

Bối cảnh: BrightWave Marketing & Advertising.

---

## Questions 1–2: Read a Text Aloud

Đọc to đoạn văn bên dưới, thu âm trong 45 giây chuẩn bị + 45 giây đọc.

#### Q1

> Welcome to BrightWave Marketing's annual Creative Showcase. Tonight, we
> celebrate the talent and creativity of our team members. Please enjoy the
> refreshments in the lobby, and feel free to explore the displays at your
> own pace. The presentation will begin promptly at six o'clock, so please
> take your seats a few minutes early.

> **Gợi ý phát âm:** nhấn trọng âm vào "Creative Showcase", "celebrate",
> "refreshments"; ngắt hơi rõ sau "lobby" và sau "own pace"; đọc "promptly at
> six o'clock" với ngữ điệu xuống để nhấn thời gian cụ thể.

#### Q2

> Attention all staff: beginning next Monday, the third-floor conference
> room will be reserved exclusively for client meetings. Team meetings and
> internal calls should be scheduled in one of the smaller meeting rooms on
> the second floor. Thank you for your cooperation as we work to make a
> better impression on our visiting clients.

> **Gợi ý phát âm:** nhấn "exclusively", "client meetings"; ngắt hơi sau
> "Attention all staff:" và sau "second floor."; giữ ngữ điệu trang trọng,
> đều, tốc độ vừa phải như một thông báo nội bộ.

## Questions 3–4: Describe a Picture

Mô tả bức tranh trong 45 giây (30 giây chuẩn bị).

#### Q3

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Một văn phòng mở (open-plan office) của công ty quảng
> cáo: ở giữa là một chiếc bàn họp lớn với bốn nhân viên đang thảo luận
> quanh laptop và các bản in mẫu quảng cáo dán trên bảng trắng phía sau. Bên
> trái, gần cửa sổ lớn, một người phụ nữ đang đứng thuyết trình chỉ vào một
> biểu đồ trên màn hình TV treo tường. Bên phải là một góc nhỏ với ghế sofa
> và cây xanh, nơi hai người khác đang trò chuyện, tay cầm cốc cà phê.

##### Gợi ý (Model answer)

In this picture, I can see an open-plan advertising office. In the center of
the room, four employees are sitting around a large table, discussing
something on a laptop while looking at printed advertisement mock-ups
pinned to a whiteboard behind them. On the left side, near a large window, a
woman appears to be giving a presentation, pointing at a chart displayed on
a wall-mounted screen. On the right side of the picture, there's a small
lounge area with a sofa and some plants, where two other people seem to be
having a casual conversation, each holding a cup of coffee. Overall, the
office looks bright, modern, and collaborative.

#### Q4

> **[Mô tả tranh]** Một sự kiện hội chợ quảng cáo (advertising trade show)
> trong một trung tâm hội nghị lớn: nhiều gian hàng (booth) với biểu ngữ
> (banner) đầy màu sắc, đại diện các công ty đứng phát tài liệu quảng cáo
> cho khách tham quan. Ở giữa lối đi, một nhóm khách đang xếp hàng để nhận
> quà tặng (túi vải in logo) tại một gian hàng lớn có biển "BrightWave
> Marketing". Phía xa là một sân khấu nhỏ nơi một diễn giả đang thuyết trình.

##### Gợi ý (Model answer)

This picture shows an advertising trade show being held inside a large
convention center. There are several colorful booths with banners, and
company representatives are handing out promotional materials to visitors
walking through the aisles. In the middle of the picture, a group of
attendees appears to be lining up to receive free gifts, possibly branded
tote bags, at a large booth with a sign that reads "BrightWave Marketing."
In the background, there seems to be a small stage where a speaker is
giving a presentation to a seated audience. The atmosphere looks busy and
energetic, which is typical of a large industry event like this.

## Questions 5–7: Respond to Questions

Chủ đề khảo sát: thói quen làm việc và quảng cáo.

#### Q5

**Question:** How often do you notice advertisements while browsing the
internet?

##### Gợi ý (Model answer)

I notice online advertisements almost every day. They usually appear on
social media or news websites.

#### Q6

**Question:** What type of advertisement do you find most memorable — video,
image, or audio?

##### Gợi ý (Model answer)

I find video advertisements the most memorable. They combine sound and
visuals, so they tend to stick in my mind longer.

#### Q7

**Question:** Describe a recent advertisement that caught your attention and
explain why it stood out to you.

##### Gợi ý (Model answer)

A recent advertisement that caught my attention was for a local coffee
shop. It used humor and a catchy jingle, which made it stand out from other
ads. The video was also very short, so it didn't feel annoying to watch.
Because of the ad, I actually visited the shop the following week, so I
think it was quite effective.

Sure. Lunch is at 1:00 p.m. After that, at 2:00 p.m., there's a Q&A panel

#### Q11 — Express an Opinion

**Question:** Some people believe that companies should spend most of their
advertising budget on social media, while others believe traditional
advertising, such as television and print, is still more effective. Which
do you think is better, and why?

##### Gợi ý (Model answer)

In my opinion, companies should focus most of their advertising budget on
social media rather than traditional advertising. First, social media
platforms allow companies to target very specific audiences based on age,
location, and interests, which makes campaigns more efficient and less
wasteful. Second, social media advertising is usually much cheaper than
television or print, especially for small businesses with limited budgets.
Finally, it's easier to measure results on social media, since companies
can track clicks, views, and engagement in real time. For these reasons, I
believe social media should be the priority, although traditional
advertising can still be useful for reaching older audiences.
